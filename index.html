Support workflow

  1. Listen: understand the customer's goal.
  2. Clarify: ask focused questions and confirm the issue.
  3. Prioritize: determine urgency and impact.
  4. Resolve: provide the simplest safe solution and guide the customer.
  5. Confirm: verify successful completion.
  6. Document: capture issue, action and outcome.
  7. Escalate: provide complete context when outside scope.
  8. Follow up: close the loop rather than assuming silence means success.

Chat support work samples

Customer:

“I’ve tried this three times and it still isn't working. This is really frustrating.”

Support response:

“I’m sorry you’ve had to repeat the process. I’ll help you work through it rather than sending you back to the same steps. First, let me confirm what you’re seeing, then I’ll check the most likely cause and give you the next step.”

Customer:

“I was charged, but I don't see the result I paid for.”

Support response:

“I understand why you’d want this checked quickly. I’ll verify the status first, then confirm what was processed and what needs to happen next. If another team is needed, I’ll include the details so you don’t have to repeat the situation.”

Customer:

“Can you just fix it? I don't understand all these technical terms.”

Support response:

“Absolutely. I’ll keep this simple. I’ll guide you through one step at a time, and you can stop me at any point if something looks different on your screen.”

Complaint handling framework

1. Acknowledge

Recognize the customer's experience without becoming defensive.

2. Own

Make it clear you are taking responsibility for moving the issue forward.

3. Investigate

Separate emotion from facts and identify the actual service problem.

4. Act

Give a specific resolution, workaround, timeframe or escalation path.

Escalation handoff template

Customer issue: What needs resolution.
Impact: What is blocked or affected.
Environment: Relevant account, device, product or workflow context.
Steps attempted: What has already been tested.
Evidence: Error, screenshot, timestamp or reference details where appropriate.
Current status: What works and what remains unresolved.
Requested action: Exactly what the receiving team needs to investigate.

Knowledge-base article sample

Customer cannot complete a website action

Symptoms: Button does not respond or process appears stuck.

First checks: Confirm browser, refresh session, test another browser/device, check visible errors, confirm account state and whether other actions work.

Verify: Ask the customer to repeat the action and confirm successful completion.

Escalate when: Standard troubleshooting fails, the issue appears account-specific, or required access is unavailable to frontline support.
